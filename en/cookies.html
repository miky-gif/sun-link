Cookies and local storage

Cookie policy

Last updated: 30 August 2026

1. In short

This website sets no cookies. No analytics, no advertising, no social media buttons, no embedded video, no remote fonts. Nothing that would normally justify a consent banner on most websites is present here.

A single piece of information is kept in your browser, and only if you click on the information banner: the fact that you have seen it, so that it is not shown again on every page.

2. What is actually stored

Name sg-cookies
Nature Local storage entry (localStorage): technically not a cookie: this value is never sent to the server, it does not leave your device.
Content A single word: accepte or refuse.
Purpose Remember that the information banner has already been shown.
Duration Until you clear your browser's site data.
Consent required No. This is storage strictly necessary for the service you requested, exempt from consent under Article 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive.

Whether you click "Accept" or "Decline" makes no difference to how the site behaves: in either case no tracker is activated, because there is none to activate. The banner informs you; it gates nothing.

3. What the website does not do

  • No analytics cookies, no Google Analytics, Matomo, Plausible or equivalent.
  • No advertising or retargeting cookies.
  • No social sharing button that sets a tracker.
  • No embedded YouTube or Vimeo video.
  • No font loaded from Google Fonts, the Sora typeface is served from this server, precisely so that no visitor's IP address is passed to a third party.
  • No profiling, no automated decision-making.

4. Server logs

Like any web server, the one hosting this site records the requests it receives: IP address, date, page requested, browser. These logs belong to the hosting provider, serve security and technical diagnostics, are not used for audience analysis, and are not cookies.

5. Contact form

The form sets no cookies. It transmits only what you type into it, at the moment you click "Send". Details of that processing are set out in the privacy policy.

6. Clearing or seeing the banner again

To make the information banner reappear, clear your browser's site data for this domain:

  • Chrome / Edge: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and site data → See all site data → search for sunygreen.com → Delete.
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data → Manage Data → search for sunygreen.com → Remove.
  • Safari: Settings → Privacy → Manage Website Data → search for sunygreen.com → Remove.

7. If this were to change

Should an analytics tool or third-party content be added, this page would be updated and the banner would become a genuine consent tool, blocking any non-essential tracker until you accept it, in accordance with Article 5(3) of the aforementioned Directive and Article 7 of the GDPR.

8. Questions

For any question about this policy, write to contact@sunygreen.com.
